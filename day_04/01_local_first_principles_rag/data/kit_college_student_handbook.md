# KIT College of Engineering & Technology
## Student Academic Regulations, Campus Policies & Code of Conduct (2025-2026)

---

## 1. Academic Regulations & Attendance Policy

### 1.1 Mandatory Attendance Threshold
- Every enrolled student must maintain a minimum cumulative attendance of **75% in each registered course** (Theory and Laboratory separately) during the semester to be eligible to appear for the Semester End Examinations (SEE).
- Attendance is recorded electronically on the KIT Student ERP portal at the end of every lecture hour.
- Attendance weightage in Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA):
  - 100% Attendance: 5 Marks
  - 90% – 99%: 4 Marks
  - 80% – 89%: 3 Marks
  - 75% – 79%: 2 Marks
  - Less than 75%: 0 Marks

### 1.2 Medical Condonation Policy (65% to 74.9%)
- Students whose attendance falls between **65.0% and 74.9%** due to genuine medical illness or hospitalization may apply for **Medical Condonation**.
- Required Procedure:
  1. Submit a valid Government-registered Medical Practitioner Certificate along with discharge summary and prescription bills to the Head of the Department (HOD) within **3 calendar days of resuming classes**.
  2. Pay a non-refundable condonation processing fee of **₹500 per subject** at the Accounts Desk.
  3. Final condonation is approved by the Dean of Academic Affairs.
- Condonation can be availed for a maximum of **two semesters** during the entire four-year degree program.

### 1.3 Detention Rule (<65%)
- Any student with attendance **below 65.0%** in any course shall be awarded the grade **'DE' (Detained)** for that course.
- Detained students are strictly barred from appearing in the Semester End Examination (SEE) for that specific subject.
- The student must re-register and re-attend all lectures for that course during the subsequent academic year whenever offered.

### 1.4 On-Duty (OD) Leave Provision
- Up to **10 working days of On-Duty (OD) leave** per semester is granted for students representing KIT in National/State Hackathons, IEEE Conferences, sports competitions, or cultural festivals.
- Prior approval from the Faculty Advisor and HOD is mandatory before departure.

## 2. Examination, Evaluation Pattern & Passing Criteria

### 2.1 Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) - 50 Marks Total
Each 4-credit course evaluation consists of two parts:
1. **Continuous Assessment Test 1 (CAT-1)**: Conducted after 5 weeks of instruction. 30 marks, scaled down to 15 marks.
2. **Continuous Assessment Test 2 (CAT-2)**: Conducted after 10 weeks of instruction. 30 marks, scaled down to 15 marks.
3. **Mini-Project or Practical Case Study**: 10 marks (evaluated by course faculty via GitHub repository / live demo).
4. **Surprise Quizzes & Assignments**: 5 marks (2 online quizzes of 2.5 marks each).
5. **Attendance Marks**: 5 marks based on Section 1.1 scale.

### 2.2 Semester End Examination (SEE) - 50 Marks Total
- Conducted as a 3-hour comprehensive written exam covering the entire syllabus.
- Evaluated out of 100 marks and scaled down to 50 marks.

### 2.3 Passing Minimum Requirements
- **SEE Passing Floor**: A student must secure a minimum of **45% marks in SEE (22.5 out of 50 marks)**.
- **Overall Aggregate**: A student must secure an aggregate of at least **50% marks (CIA + SEE $\ge$ 50 out of 100)** to successfully pass the course and earn course credits.
- If a student scores >50 overall but fails to get 22.5/50 in SEE, the student is awarded an 'RA' (Re-Appear / Arrear) grade.

## 3. Central Library & Digital E-Resource Guidelines

### 3.1 Book Borrowing Quota and Duration
- **Undergraduate (B.Tech / B.E.) Students**: May borrow up to **4 physical books** simultaneously using their RFID Smart Identity Card.
- **Postgraduate (M.Tech / M.S.) Students**: May borrow up to **6 physical books**.
- Standard loan period is **14 calendar days**.
- Renewal is permitted once for an additional 7 days if no other student has placed a hold/reservation on the title.

### 3.2 Overdue Fines and Damage Penalties
- Overdue fine of **₹5 per day per book** for the first 7 days after the due date.
- Overdue fine increases to **₹10 per day per book** starting from the 8th day until returned.
- Loss or defacement of a library book incurs replacement of the identical latest edition plus a 20% administrative handling charge.

### 3.3 Digital Research Databases & Remote Access
- The library subscribes to IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, SpringerLink, Elsevier ScienceDirect, and Harvard Business Publishing.
- 24x7 off-campus remote access is enabled through **OpenAthens Single Sign-On (SSO)** using the student's official `@kit.ac.in` college email ID.
- AI research tools and computing terminals are located on the 2nd Floor Digital Commons (Open 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM).

## 4. Training, Internships & Campus Placement Regulations

### 4.1 Eligibility Criteria for Campus Recruitment
- **General Registration Cutoff**: Students entering the 6th semester must have a cumulative **CGPA of 7.00 or higher** with **zero standing/active arrears (backlogs)** at the time of registration.
- **Tier-1 Dream Companies (CTC $\ge$ ₹10.0 LPA)**: Require CGPA $\ge$ 8.00, 10th and 12th board marks $\ge$ 75%, and a verified competitive coding profile (LeetCode / Codeforces).
- **Placement Training Attendance**: Minimum **85% attendance** is mandatory in Aptitude, Soft Skills, and Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA) training bootcamps organized by the Career Development Cell (CDC).

### 4.2 The "One-Offer" and "Dream Offer" Policy
- Once a student secures a campus job offer with a CTC of **₹5.0 LPA to ₹9.99 LPA**, they are categorized as "Placed" and are excused from regular company drives.
- Exception: A placed student is permitted to apply for up to **two "Dream Companies" (CTC $\ge$ ₹10.0 LPA)** or any **"Super Dream Company" (CTC $\ge$ ₹18.0 LPA)**.
- If a student receives a Dream Offer, that becomes their final accepted offer.

### 4.3 Mandatory Summer Internship
- Every student must complete a minimum of **8 weeks of industry internship** between the 6th and 7th semesters.
- Research internships under recognized IIT/IISc professors or accredited research labs are also accepted.
- A final internship viva-voce is conducted in the 7th semester carrying **2 academic credits**.

## 5. Hostel Accommodation, Mess Timings & Campus Life

### 5.1 Hostel Gate Curfew & Security Protocol
- Main campus gates and hostel entry doors close strictly at **8:30 PM** every night.
- Biometric facial recognition punch-in is mandatory before 8:30 PM.
- Late entry up to **9:30 PM** is permitted ONLY with an electronic gate pass issued by the Chief Warden for students utilizing Central Library or Innovation Labs.
- Unexcused absence after 9:30 PM generates an automated SMS alert to registered parents/guardians and incurs a ₹1,000 disciplinary fine.

### 5.2 Mess Facilities & Timings
- Breakfast: 7:15 AM – 8:45 AM
- Lunch: 12:15 PM – 1:45 PM
- Evening Tea & Snacks: 4:30 PM – 5:30 PM
- Dinner: 7:30 PM – 9:00 PM
- Dietary changes or complaints should be lodged through the Student Mess Committee portal.

### 5.3 Anti-Ragging Absolute Zero Tolerance Policy
- KIT enforces a **Zero-Tolerance Anti-Ragging Policy** in strict compliance with the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India guidelines and UGC Regulations.
- Any form of physical harassment, verbal abuse, cyber-bullying, or forceful conduct results in:
  1. Immediate suspension pending enquiry.
  2. Expulsion from the college and hostel upon prima facie evidence.
  3. Lodging of an official First Information Report (FIR) with local Police authorities within 24 hours.
- 24x7 Anti-Ragging Helpline: Call `1800-180-5522` or email `antiragging@kit.ac.in`.

## 6. Fee Payment Schedules, Fines & Merit Scholarships

### 6.1 Semester Fee Payment Deadline
- Tuition and hostel fees must be paid within **10 working days** from the commencement of the semester.
- A late fine of **₹100 per day** is levied from the 11th working day up to the 25th working day.
- Hall tickets for semester examinations will NOT be generated if fee dues remain outstanding.

### 6.2 Founder's Merit Scholarship Scheme
- **Semester Toppers (Rank 1 in Department)**: Awarded **50% Tuition Fee Waiver** for the subsequent semester provided CGPA is $\ge$ 9.25 with no history of disciplinary penalties.
- **Merit-Cum-Means Scholarship**: ₹25,000 annual scholarship for students with family income $< ₹3,00,000$ per annum and CGPA $\ge$ 8.00.
