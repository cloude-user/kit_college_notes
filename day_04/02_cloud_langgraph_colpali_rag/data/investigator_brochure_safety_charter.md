# INVESTIGATOR BROCHURE & CLINICAL SAFETY CHARTER: ABX-702
**AuraBio Therapeutics Clinical Development Division · Document ID: IB-ABX702-ED7**
**Edition: 7.0 · Effective Date: January 15, 2025 · Sponsor Safety Committee Charter**

---

## 1. MECHANISM OF ACTION & TARGET PHARMACODYNAMICS
ABX-702 is a first-in-class humanized bispecific monoclonal IgG1 antibody engineered with dual high-affinity binding domains targeting:
1. **T-cell Immunoreceptor with Ig and ITIM Domains (TIGIT)** ($K_D = 0.32\text{ nM}$)
2. **Programmed Cell Death Protein 1 (PD-1)** ($K_D = 0.18\text{ nM}$)

### Fc-Silenced Effector Function
ABX-702 incorporates an engineered `L234A/L235A (LALA)` mutation in the Fc region to abolish antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC) and complement-dependent cytotoxicity (CDC), preventing unwanted depletion of activated CD8+ tumor-infiltrating effector lymphocytes and regulatory T cells (Tregs).

## 2. ADVERSE EVENTS OF SPECIAL INTEREST (AESI)
Because ABX-702 co-inhibits two negative immune checkpoint regulators, clinical investigators must remain vigilant for immune-mediated adverse reactions:

### A. Immune-Mediated Pneumonitis
- **Incidence Benchmark**: Observed in 3.8% of patients receiving anti-PD-1/TIGIT bispecific therapies.
- **Signs & Symptoms**: New or worsening dyspnea, non-productive cough, chest pain, or focal ground-glass opacities on high-resolution chest CT.
- **Management Algorithm**:
  - *Grade 1 (Asymptomatic, radiographic only)*: Continue therapy with bi-weekly pulse oximetry monitoring.
  - *Grade 2 (Symptomatic, limiting instrumental ADLs)*: Hold ABX-702. Initiate oral prednisone 1 to 2 mg/kg/day. Taper over minimum 4 to 6 weeks.
  - *Grade 3–4 (Severe hypoxia, oxygen required)*: Permanently discontinue ABX-702. Hospitalize patient immediately. Administer IV methylprednisolone 2 to 4 mg/kg/day. If no improvement within 48 hours, add infliximab 5 mg/kg or mycophenolate mofetil 1 g BID.

### B. Immune-Mediated Hepatotoxicity
- **Incidence Benchmark**: ALT or AST elevations $> 3\times\text{ ULN}$ occur in 6.2% of trial subjects.
- **Dose Adjustment**:
  - AST/ALT $3.0\text{ to }5.0\times\text{ ULN}$ or Total Bilirubin $1.5\text{ to }3.0\times\text{ ULN}$: Withhold dose until $\le 1.5\times\text{ ULN}$.
  - AST/ALT $> 5.0\times\text{ ULN}$ or Total Bilirubin $> 3.0\times\text{ ULN}$: **Permanently discontinue**. Administer systemic corticosteroids.

### C. Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS)
- **Clinical Presentation**: Pyrexia $\ge 38.0^\circ\text{C}$, rigors, hypotension, tachypnea, and transient hypoxemia occurring within 24 hours of infusion.
- **Therapeutic Intervention**: Tocilizumab (IL-6 receptor antagonist) 8 mg/kg IV (max 800 mg) infused over 60 minutes for Grade $\ge 2$ CRS. Repeat after 8 hours if hemodynamically unstable.

## 3. CLINICAL INVESTIGATOR RESPONSIBILITIES & INDEMNIFICATION
- **Site Sponsor Indemnity Cap**: AuraBio maintains a comprehensive clinical trial liability policy providing **$25,000,000** aggregate indemnity coverage per participating university hospital or clinical research institution.
- **Investigational Product Storage**: Store vials of ABX-702 concentrate ($20\text{ mg/mL}$) refrigerated at $2^\circ\text{C to }8^\circ\text{C}$ ($36^\circ\text{F to }46^\circ\text{F}$) in original cartons protected from light. Do not freeze or shake.
- **Drug Accountability**: Drug dispensing logs (`FORM-CLIN-DISP-04`) must record vial serial numbers, lot numbers, reconstitution volumes, infusion bag timestamps, and unused drug destruction certificates.
