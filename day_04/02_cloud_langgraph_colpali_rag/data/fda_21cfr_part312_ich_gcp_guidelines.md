## 1. 21 CFR § 312.32: EXPEDITED IND SAFETY REPORTING MANDATES
The Sponsor must notify the FDA and all participating clinical investigators in an IND safety report of potential serious and unexpected adverse reactions:

### A. 7-Day Expedited Alert (Fatal or Life-Threatening SUSAR)
- **Regulatory Citation**: `21 CFR § 312.32(c)(2)`
- **Trigger**: Any unexpected fatal or life-threatening suspected adverse reaction associated with the use of the investigational drug.
- **Reporting Window**: The Sponsor must notify the FDA as soon as possible, but in no case later than **7 calendar days** after the Sponsor's initial receipt of the safety event information.
- **Transmission Method**: Form FDA 3500A (MedWatch) transmitted via FDA Electronic Submissions Gateway (ESG) or direct telephone/facsimile alerting the CDER review division.
- **Follow-up Obligation**: Comprehensive written narrative and complete hospital records must be submitted within an additional **8 calendar days** (Total 15 days).

### B. 15-Day Expedited Written Safety Report
- **Regulatory Citation**: `21 CFR § 312.32(c)(1)`
- **Trigger**: Any serious and unexpected suspected adverse reaction (SUSAR) that does not meet the fatal/life-threatening threshold, clinically important increased rate of serious suspected adverse reactions over baseline, or meaningful finding from animal carcinogenicity/mutagenicity studies.
- **Reporting Window**: The Sponsor must submit a formal written IND safety report to the FDA and all clinical trial investigators no later than **15 calendar days** after the Sponsor determines that the information qualifies for reporting.

## 2. SERIOUS ADVERSE EVENT (SAE) DEFINITION CRITERIA
Per `21 CFR § 312.32(a)`, an adverse event or suspected adverse reaction is classified as **SERIOUS** if, in the view of either the investigator or sponsor, it results in any of the following outcomes:
1. **Death**.
2. **A life-threatening adverse event** (patient was at immediate risk of death from the event as it occurred).
3. **Inpatient hospitalization or prolongation of existing hospitalization** ($\ge 24\text{ hours}$).
4. **A persistent or significant incapacity or substantial disruption of the ability to conduct normal life functions**.
5. **A congenital anomaly / birth defect**.
6. **Important Medical Events (IME)**: Medical events that may not result in death, be life-threatening, or require hospitalization may be considered serious when, based upon appropriate medical judgment, they may jeopardize the patient and may require medical or surgical intervention to prevent one of the outcomes listed above.

## 3. CLINICAL TRIAL STOPPING RULES & IND SAFETY HOLDS
Under ICH GCP E6(R2) section 5.20 and FDA 21 CFR § 312.44, mandatory study pauses and clinical holds are executed under the following conditions:

- **Automatic Safety Hold Rule A**: Any single treatment-related death (Grade 5 CTCAE) attributable to study drug ABX-702 halts enrollment across all sites pending Safety Review Committee (SRC) determination.
- **Automatic Safety Hold Rule B**: Occurrence of Grade 4 treatment-related non-hematologic toxicity in $\ge 15\%$ of evaluable patients within any individual dose cohort requires immediate cessation of dose escalation.
- **Independent Data Monitoring Committee (IDMC)**: The IDMC convenes semi-annually or ad-hoc within **48 hours** of any reported cluster of $\ge 2$ Immune-Mediated Serious Adverse Events (imSAEs).

## 4. 21 CFR PART 11 COMPLIANCE: AUDIT TRAILS & ELECTRONIC SIGNATURES
All clinical data capture, adverse event recording, and protocol management systems must adhere to strict electronic verification:
1. **WORM Storage**: Electronic Trial Master File (eTMF) and Electronic Data Capture (EDC) systems must maintain write-once-read-many immutable audit trails.
2. **Audit Log Retention**: Audit logs recording every data insertion, modification, deletion, and timestamped user ID must be retained for **25 years** post-licensure or 2 years post-discontinuation of clinical investigations.
3. **Dual Sign-Off Mandate**: Any medical data amendment, adverse event causality reassessment, or unblinding event requires dual sign-off from the Principal Investigator (PI) and Sponsor Medical Monitor.
