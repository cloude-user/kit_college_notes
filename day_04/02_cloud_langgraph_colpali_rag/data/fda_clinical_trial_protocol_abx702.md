# FDA CLINICAL TRIAL PROTOCOL: ABX-702-CLIN-301
**AuraBio Therapeutics Inc. · Investigational New Drug (IND) Application No. 142,889**
**Phase III Randomized, Double-Blind, Multi-Center Study of ABX-702 (Dual TIGIT / PD-1 Bispecific Antibody) in Patients with Metastatic Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)**

---

## 1. PROTOCOL ADMINISTRATIVE SUMMARY
- **Protocol Identifier**: `ABX-702-CLIN-301`
- **IND Number**: `IND-142889`
- **EudraCT Number**: `2024-001928-34`
- **Sponsor**: AuraBio Therapeutics Inc., South San Francisco, CA
- **Phase**: Phase III Registrational Confirmatory Trial
- **Target Population**: Histologically confirmed Stage IV or recurrent metastatic Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) with PD-L1 Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) $\ge 1\%$.
- **Primary Endpoint**: Overall Survival (OS) and Progression-Free Survival (PFS) by Blinded Independent Central Review (BICR) per RECIST v1.1.
- **Secondary Endpoints**: Objective Response Rate (ORR), Duration of Response (DoR), Pharmacokinetics (PK), Safety & Tolerability.

---

## 2. DOSING COHORTS & ADMINISTRATION SCHEDULE
Treatment is administered via intravenous (IV) infusion over 60 minutes ($\pm 10$ minutes) on Day 1 of each 21-day cycle (Cycle Length: 21 days / Q3W).

| Cohort | Assigned Regimen | Dose Level | Infusion Frequency | Dose Limiting Toxicity (DLT) Window | Target Enrollment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cohort 1** | Monotherapy Low-Dose | `150 mg IV` | Q3W (Every 21 Days) | Cycle 1, Day 1 to Day 21 | 60 Patients |
| **Cohort 2** | Monotherapy Target Dose | `300 mg IV` | Q3W (Every 21 Days) | Cycle 1, Day 1 to Day 21 | 180 Patients |
| **Cohort 3** | High-Dose Exploratory | `450 mg IV` | Q3W (Every 21 Days) | Cycle 1, Day 1 to Day 21 | 80 Patients |
| **Cohort 4** | Combination + Chemotherapy | `300 mg IV + Carboplatin/Pemetrexed` | Q3W for 4 cycles, then 300 mg maint. | Cycle 1, Day 1 to Day 21 | 120 Patients |

### Pre-Medication Requirements
- Diphenhydramine 50 mg IV (or oral equivalent) administered 30–60 minutes prior to infusion.
- Acetaminophen 650–1000 mg orally 30–60 minutes prior to infusion.
- Dexamethasone 10 mg IV for patients who previously exhibited Grade $\ge 1$ infusion-related reactions (IRR).

---

## 3. DOSE MODIFICATION & TOXICITY MANAGEMENT MATRIX
Dose escalations within an individual patient are strictly prohibited. Dose reductions are permanent; re-escalation is not permitted.

| CTCAE Toxicity Grade | Action on ABX-702 Study Drug | Dose Level Adjustment | Clinical Management Mandate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grade 1** | Continue at current dose level | No change | Symptomatic management as indicated. |
| **Grade 2 (General)** | Hold dose until resolution to Grade $\le 1$ | Resume at same dose level (300 mg) | Supportive care. If unresolved in 14 days, reduce 1 dose tier. |
| **Grade 2 (Pneumonitis)** | Withhold study drug immediately | Reduce to Tier 1 (`200 mg`) upon recovery | Corticosteroid therapy: Prednisone 1 mg/kg/day tapered over 4 weeks. |
| **Grade 3 (Non-Hematologic)** | Withhold study drug immediately | Reduce to Tier 2 (`100 mg`) upon recovery | High-dose IV methylprednisolone 1–2 mg/kg/day until Grade $\le 1$. |
| **Grade 4 (Life-Threatening)** | **Permanently Discontinue Study Drug** | Off-Study Treatment | Immediate hospitalization, ICU monitoring, expedited FDA 7-day notification. |

*Dose Tier Reductions*:
- Starting Target Dose: `300 mg`
- Dose Reduction Tier -1: `200 mg`
- Dose Reduction Tier -2: `100 mg`
- Below Tier -2: Permanent study drug discontinuation.

---

## 4. PHARMACOKINETIC (PK) PROFILE & LABORATORY MONITORING
Steady-state pharmacokinetics are established following the third cycle of administration (Week 9):
- **Maximum Serum Concentration ($C_{\text{max}}$)**: $84.5\text{ }\mu\text{g/mL}$ ($\pm 12.3$) at 300 mg Q3W.
- **Area Under Curve ($\text{AUC}_{0-\infty}$)**: $1,420\text{ }\mu\text{g}\cdot\text{h/mL}$.
- **Terminal Elimination Half-Life ($t_{1/2}$)**: $18.4\text{ days}$ (allowing 21-day Q3W sustained target engagement).
- **Volume of Distribution ($V_d$)**: $4.8\text{ L}$ (consistent with limited extravascular distribution typical of bispecific IgG1 constructs).

### Scheduled Clinical Assessments
1. **Safety Laboratory Panel** (Hematology, Coagulation, Comprehensive Metabolic Panel, Troponin-I):
   - Baseline (within 72 hours of C1D1).
   - Pre-dose on Day 1 of every cycle.
   - Mid-cycle Day 8 ($\pm 1$ day) during Cycles 1 and 2.
2. **Tumor Imaging (CT Chest/Abdomen/Pelvis per RECIST v1.1)**:
   - Baseline within 28 days prior to randomization.
   - Every 6 weeks ($\pm 7$ days) through Week 24.
   - Every 9 weeks thereafter until confirmed disease progression or consent withdrawal.
